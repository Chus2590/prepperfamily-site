      How to play

      • Tap any letters you want, in any order: all of them work, they don't need to be neighbors. Form a word of 3 letters or more.
      • Press Attack! to throw your word at the villain (or Save word when there is no villain): the letters go away and new ones fall in.
      • ⚔️ Damage: each letter in your word deals 1 damage to the villain; each golden acorn adds +2. Long words = strong hits!
      • ⚡ Superpower: once in a while a lightning tile appears: it doubles your word's damage and gives +50 points. 🛡️ Some villains are immune to 3-letter words: against them, use 4 or more.
      • The golden letters are acorns: they are worth double points. Words of 6 letters or more give +50.
      • 🔒 Locked letters: you can't tap them. Use a key 🔑 inside a word to free them (they sometimes hide acorns).
      • 🐭 Villains: from the second world on, a thief wants to rob your pantry. If you don't defeat it, it counterattacks every 4 words by infecting a letter (keys cure them). When you defeat it: +150 points, +1 hint and +1 shuffle.
      • Each word in a row adds a streak bonus. Reach each world's goal to advance.
      • 🏆 Your records (best scores and best words) are saved only on your device.
      • 🎵 The music and sound effects start with your first tap; the 🎵 and 🔊 buttons mute them separately.
