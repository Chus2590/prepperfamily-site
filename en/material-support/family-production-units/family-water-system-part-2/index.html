Storage and water purification system

Once you have the 3000 ppm CDS concentrate in your refrigerator, the next step is to ensure that the water stored in your reserves is fit for human consumption by eliminating bacteria, viruses, fungi, and parasites through selective oxidation.

1 · Container selection and preparation

Successful storage depends on preventing algae growth and material corrosion.

  • Primary containers: use black or opaque high-density polyethylene (HDPE) barrels. Opacity is critical for blocking UV rays, which break down chlorine dioxide and promote algae growth.
  • Extraction system: shutoff valves must be made strictly of plastic (PVC or PP).
⚠️ No metals

Never use metal valves or pipes: ClO₂ will corrode the material and contaminate the water with toxic metal residue.

2 · The “golden rule” of water purification

3 · Biofilm management and maintenance

Over time, the walls of the barrels may develop biofilm, a slimy bacterial layer. Unlike ordinary chlorine, CDS has the unique ability to penetrate and dismantle this structure.

  • Shock cleaning: if you notice sediment or slime when inspecting your barrels, add 5 ml of CDS per liter of capacity and leave it to work for 24 hours before rinsing and refilling.
  • Thermal stability: ClO₂ gas remains better dissolved in cold water. If the temperature of the treated water rises above 11 °C, the gas may gradually evaporate and reduce its long-term disinfecting power.

4 · Consumption safety

Harmless residue

After the oxidation reaction, the final residue in the water is oxygen and a minimal amount of common salt (sodium chloride), which is completely harmless to the body.

Final protection: for already purified water that will be moved to the table or used daily, choose amber glass bottles or HDPE containers to protect it from ambient light.

The emergency chemical kit

In an emergency or isolation situation, 3000 ppm CDS becomes a multipurpose tool that can replace a wide variety of conventional medications. These are the protocols for common incidents requiring a rapid response.

H

Preventing transmission · air disinfection

Vital when a family member has a respiratory illness and you want to prevent others from becoming infected. It is based on the slow release of ClO₂ gas into the environment.

  • Procedure: activate 6 to 12 drops of sodium chlorite and activator in a dry glass, without adding water.
  • Placement: place the glass 1 to 2 meters away from the sick person.
  • Regulation: if you want the gas to be released more slowly and last throughout the night, add one tablespoon of water to the mixture.
  • Effect: the evaporated gas eliminates airborne pathogens before they are inhaled by other people.
Q

Burns and tissue regeneration

CDS is exceptionally effective for treating burns: it eliminates pain almost instantly and accelerates tissue recovery without leaving deep scars.

  1. Cooling: first wash the burn with plenty of cold water to stop the thermal damage.
  2. Application: spray undiluted 3000 ppm CDS directly onto the affected area.
  3. Frequency: if the pain returns after a few minutes, spray again every 30 minutes until the discomfort stops completely.

Generally, one to three treatments are enough to stabilize the area and regenerate the tissue quickly.

I

Stings and bites

It is used to neutralize toxins injected by insects, spiders, jellyfish, and even ticks.

  • Immediate procedure: soak gauze or cotton with cold 3000 ppm CDS from the refrigerator and apply it directly to the sting or bite.
  • Action of the gas: ClO₂ penetrates the wound and oxidizes the acidic proteins in the venom, neutralizing its toxic effect immediately.
  • Severe cases: for bites from venomous spiders (such as the Chilean recluse) or stings from scorpions, it can be combined with Protocol K by adding DMSO so that the purifier quickly reaches greater depths.
J

Oral hygiene and infection prevention

In environments without access to a dentist, CDS prevents serious infections, eliminates dental biofilm, and heals canker sores in record time.

  • Standard rinse: dilute 10 ml of CDS in a glass containing 200 ml of water.
  • Application: gargle and rinse for one minute, ensuring that the liquid reaches the entire mouth.
  • Benefits: it is a lethal treatment for gingivitis (bleeding gums) and pyorrhea, and eliminates bad breath at its source by destroying anaerobic bacteria.

In situations of scarcity or lack of electric refrigeration, CDS is the best ally for food safety.
