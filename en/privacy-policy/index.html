Privacy Policy

At PrepperFamily.org we respect your privacy. This site is an informational resource for families: we do not ask for your personal information, and we do not collect it.

PrepperFamily.org has no user accounts, forms, or comments. We do not ask for your name, your email address, or any personal information, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your phone

If you install the app or save guides, the app stores your saved guides and reading progress only on your own phone, in the browser's local storage. That information never leaves your phone and is never sent to any server. You can delete it by removing your saved guides, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

When you use certain links and services you leave PrepperFamily.org, and those services' policies apply:

  • Donations. Donations are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects the payment information; PrepperFamily.org does not receive your card details.
  • Affiliate links to Amazon. Clicking takes you to Amazon, which may use cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy.
  • Store. The store runs on Fourthwall, which processes orders.
  • Social media. Links to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Pinterest take you to those platforms.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts; when it does, Google may receive your IP address.

Children

The site is written for families, but it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is the one you see on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through PrepperFamily.org.
